Single-window alerts betray you: too tight and they page on noise, too loose and they
miss real burn. Multi-window multi-burn-rate fixes that and almost nobody hand-writes
the rules correctly, because the arithmetic is fiddly. That's exactly the shape of
problem worth compiling.
