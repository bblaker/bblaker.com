Three machines, all running Klipper, all modified past the point where the original
manual is useful. This page is the fleet's home: every repair, mod, tuning session and
failure gets a devlog entry here rather than disappearing into a notes file.

## The standing setup

Everything is on Klipper with Moonraker exposed to the network, which means printers
are just another thing on the cluster's monitoring — chamber temperatures and print
state land in the same Prometheus as the servers do. That turned out to be the single
best quality-of-life change I've made.

## What I actually spend time on

Not printing. First layers, belt tension, and the slow drift of a machine that's been
running for a year. The mods that stuck are the boring ones.
